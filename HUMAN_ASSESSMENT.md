# Human Assessment

Human assessment for *Eval4MHSim: A Persona Collection and Evaluation Framework for Mental Health User Simulation in Social Media*. It complements the automatic evaluation on a subset of the test instances and is provided for reference and reproducibility.

## A. Design

The assessment compares replies of 12B POWER-ICL, the highest-scoring configuration, with replies of two other configurations of the same model for the same user and post:

| Comparison | Difference between the configurations |
|---|---|
| POWER-ICL vs P-ICL | POWER vs the profile-based persona, with the same examples from the target user |
| POWER-ICL vs POWER-ZS | Same-user examples vs none, with the same POWER persona |

**Instances.** 50 test instances were sampled at random (seed 42), one per user, from the 63-user evaluation cohort. Posts longer than 250 words, original replies shorter than 8 or longer than 120 words, and instances with suicide, self-harm or overdose content were excluded before sampling. Each instance appears in both comparisons. Usernames, subreddit names and links were removed from the texts shown.

**Task.** Each item shows the post, the user's original reply and the two simulated replies. The simulated replies are shown in full, side by side, as Reply A and Reply B. The position of POWER-ICL is balanced within each group of items. Each study asks one forced-choice question:

| Question | Wording |
|---|---|
| Tone | Which reply is closer to the original reply in emotional tone? |
| Identity | Which reply is more likely written by the same person as the original reply? |

**Studies.** There are four studies, one per comparison and question, run on the Prolific AI Task Builder. Each study splits the 50 items into five groups of 10. Each group is one submission and is completed by three annotators. Each submission also contains two instructed attention checks, in which one reply is replaced by "This is an attention check. Please choose this reply." The studies were published one after another, and each excluded the participants of all previous studies. No annotator saw an instance twice or answered both questions.

**Annotators.** Annotators were Prolific participants with English as first language, an approval rate of at least 98%, at least 200 previous submissions, and willingness to see potentially upsetting content. The studies carried a sensitive-content warning, and participants gave informed consent. Payment was £1.20 to £1.50 per submission.

**Attention checks.** 61 annotators took part in the main studies. 60 passed both checks. One failed both and was replaced by a new annotator on the same group. The analysis uses the 600 judgments of the 60 annotators who passed, three per item.

**Pilot.** Before the main studies, three annotators answered both questions on one group of 10 items from the POWER-ICL vs P-ICL comparison. The median completion time was 8.1 minutes. Each annotator gave the same answer to both questions on 80% to 100% of the items, so the main studies ask one question each. Pilot judgments are not analysed or released.

**Time.** Median completion times were 8.8 minutes (POWER-ICL vs P-ICL, tone), 10.2 (POWER-ICL vs P-ICL, identity), 7.8 (POWER-ICL vs POWER-ZS, tone) and 8.9 (POWER-ICL vs POWER-ZS, identity).

## B. Instructions

Annotators read the following before the task:

> Each item shows a Reddit **post**, the **original reply** a user wrote to it, and two other replies, **A** and **B**. For each item, answer one question:
>
> **Which reply is closer to the original reply in emotional tone?** Consider the feelings expressed (for example joy, sadness, anger, fear, surprise, disgust, or none), not the topic or how well it is written.
>
> If unsure, choose the closer one. Some items contain an instruction in place of a reply. Follow it.

The identity studies replace the question and its guidance with:

> **Which reply is more likely written by the same person as the original reply?** Consider writing style, vocabulary, length, and what this person would say.

After the task, annotators were told that most replies were generated by language models.

## C. Results

**Table C1.** Preference for POWER-ICL over 50 items and 150 judgments per setting. Win rate is the share of judgments choosing POWER-ICL. Majority is the share of items where at least two of the three annotators chose POWER-ICL. Items won/lost counts the items where the majority chose or did not choose POWER-ICL. Unanimous won/lost counts the items where all three annotators chose POWER-ICL or all three chose the other configuration.

| Comparison | Question | Win rate | Majority | Items won/lost | Unanimous won/lost |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| POWER-ICL vs P-ICL | Tone | 0.55 | 52% | 26/24 | 13/7 |
| POWER-ICL vs P-ICL | Identity | 0.57 | 64% | 32/18 | 10/7 |
| POWER-ICL vs POWER-ZS | Tone | 0.54 | 56% | 28/22 | 7/4 |
| POWER-ICL vs POWER-ZS | Identity | 0.68 | 76% | 38/12 | 20/6 |

Annotators preferred POWER-ICL in all four settings. In each setting the win rate is above 0.5, and POWER-ICL wins more items than it loses, by majority and by unanimous vote. The preference is strongest for identity against POWER-ZS, where POWER-ICL wins 38 of 50 items and all three annotators chose it on 20.

**Table C2.** Annotators by the share of their 10 items on which they chose POWER-ICL. Each setting has 15 annotators.

| Comparison | Question | More than half | Exactly half | Fewer than half |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| POWER-ICL vs P-ICL | Tone | 9 | 3 | 3 |
| POWER-ICL vs P-ICL | Identity | 8 | 2 | 5 |
| POWER-ICL vs POWER-ZS | Tone | 6 | 5 | 4 |
| POWER-ICL vs POWER-ZS | Identity | 12 | 2 | 1 |

In each setting, more annotators chose POWER-ICL on more than half of their items than on fewer than half. For identity against POWER-ZS, 12 of 15 annotators did so.

Reply A was chosen in 51.7% of the 600 judgments.

## D. Agreement

Agreement among the three annotators of each item was highest for identity against POWER-ZS: 68% of annotator pairs chose the same configuration, all three agreed on 52% of the items, and Gwet's AC1 was 0.43. AC1 uses the chosen configuration as the category and corrects chance agreement for the skew in the answers.

## E. Comparison with Eval4MHSim

**Ranking.** The human preferences are consistent with the overall Eval4MHSim scores (POWER-ICL 0.983, P-ICL 0.914, POWER-ZS 0.900). Annotators preferred POWER-ICL in both comparisons. The majority chose POWER-ICL on more items against POWER-ZS than against P-ICL, for both questions (identity 76% vs 64%, tone 56% vs 52%).

**Emotion classifier.** For each tone item, the emotion classifier of the emotionality dimension (`j-hartmann/emotion-english-distilroberta-base`) selects the reply whose emotion distribution is closest to that of the original reply, measured by Jensen–Shannon divergence. The annotator majority chose the same reply on 62% of the tone items, 56% against P-ICL and 68% against POWER-ZS.

## F. Released Data

The judgments are released without any text. Instance identifiers match those of the gated evaluation release on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/irlab-udc/erisk-depression-personas), which contains the simulated replies. Reddit posts and original replies are not released. Annotator identifiers are anonymous and unrelated to Prolific IDs.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `human_assessment/judgments.csv` | One row per judgment: `instance`, `comparison`, `question`, `annotator`, `reply_a` and `reply_b` (the configurations shown in each position), `choice`, and `valid` (false for the annotator excluded after failing both attention checks) |
| `human_assessment/items.csv` | One row per item: `instance`, `comparison`, and `classifier_closer` (the configuration whose reply the emotion classifier places closest to the original) |

The attention-check counts in Section A and the human assessment results in Sections C to E can be recomputed from the released files with:

```bash
python src/e4s/human/analyze.py --data human_assessment
```

The script also fits a mixed-effects logistic regression with random intercepts for item and annotator. The p-value reported in the paper and the other significance tests come from this model.
